Kunal Enterprises

Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

This policy explains how Kunal Enterprises handles personal information when customers and their representatives contact us on WhatsApp about orders or inquiries, and when our sales team uses WhatsApp or the order portal for that work.

Information we collect

  • Names, phone numbers, business affiliations and account details used to identify customers, their representatives and sales employees.
  • WhatsApp messages and replies, message timestamps, and any photos, documents, contact cards or locations you share.
  • Order and inquiry details, such as items, quantities, dispatch warehouses, delivery addresses and delivery contacts.
  • Portal sign-in and activity records, such as changes and approvals made on an order request.

If you share another person's details, provide only what is needed for the order or delivery and make sure you have permission to share it.

How we use information

We use this information to identify the customer, respond to inquiries, prepare and review order requests, arrange dispatch and delivery, and maintain order records. We may notify assigned sales employees about customer orders. We also use relevant records to troubleshoot errors, protect accounts and investigate misuse.

When AI-assisted intake is enabled, relevant messages, attachments, sender and order context, and selected examples from past requests that staff reviewed may be sent to OpenAI or to OpenRouter and its selected model provider. This helps identify order details. Staff can review and correct the resulting requests.

Who receives information

We share information as needed with staff handling your order, delivery providers, and service providers that help us run the portal, including hosting and AI providers where used. WhatsApp and Meta process messages as part of providing WhatsApp. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Retention and access

We keep information for as long as needed to handle inquiries, fulfill orders, resolve disputes and meet legal or accounting obligations. Portal access is controlled through user accounts and role-based permissions.

Questions and requests

Contact Kunal Enterprises through the business WhatsApp number you use for orders, or ask your usual Kunal Enterprises representative to route your request. You can raise a privacy question or complaint, ask to access, correct or delete your information where applicable, or ask us to stop optional WhatsApp notifications. We may ask for enough information to confirm your identity and locate the relevant records.

We post updates to this policy on this page.
